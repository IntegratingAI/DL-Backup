Cybersecurity

Two ways in: Learning builds the concepts from your first command to confident defender, and Practice is hands-on reps — Net Drills for subnetting and Cisco IOS, Lockdown Drills for hardening Windows, Linux, FreeBSD, and Cisco IOS, and Lockdown Range for full hardening labs that run in your browser.

Unofficial practice; not affiliated with or endorsed by Microsoft, Canonical, Linux Mint, the FreeBSD Project, or Cisco. Defensive skills only: practice on machines you own or are authorized to use. Progress stays in your browser.

Learning

Build the concepts and vocabulary, from your first terminal command to confident defender. New to cybersecurity? Start with Defense Ladder, the course. The Study Map finds any concept as you type and shows where to learn and practice it.

  • Defense Ladder

    Start here: the course, one grade at a time.

    A free, four-grade cybersecurity course, Freshman to Senior, built on Bloom's taxonomy: 54 units across Windows, Linux, networking, security foundations, defense operations, and web and cloud, with diagrams, drills on realistic logs, captures, and configs, a readiness check for each grade, and a glossary. Works offline, and everything stays in your browser.

Practice

Hands-on reps once you know the ideas. Net Drills are timed networking drills. Lockdown Drills walk you through hardening each system step by step. Lockdown Range drops you into a full simulated machine to harden on your own, with a live Progress Check.

Net Drills4 tools

Four networking drills: subnetting and IPv6, Cisco IOS configuration, config audits, and troubleshooting from real device output. They pair well with Cisco networking courses such as the NetAcad Network Technician path.

  • Subnet Sprint

    Calculate it. Then faster.

    Slide the mask in the sandbox and watch every value react, then work out network, broadcast, and host ranges against the clock. Includes VLSM planning and IPv6 drills.
